Beam: Quiet Circuits — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026. Beam: Quiet Circuits is the name on the App Store; the app appears as Beam on your home screen. This policy covers both, because they are the same app.

Beam collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network code of any kind.

The only thing it stores is your progress — which circuits you have finished, in how many turns, and your sound, haptics, motion and palette settings. That file lives on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be sent. Deleting the app deletes it.

Beam is not directed at children specifically, and collects no data from anyone regardless of age.

If this ever changes — if a future version adds anything that leaves the device — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than implied.
