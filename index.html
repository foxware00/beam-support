Beam: Quiet Circuits

A quiet puzzle about light: rotate the pieces to route coloured beams from their sources to the targets.

Beam: Quiet Circuits is the name on the App Store; the app itself appears as Beam on your home screen.

Support

Something broken, or an idea? Email foxwaredev@gmail.com.

Progress

Beam keeps your progress on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it, and there is no account to restore it from.
